---
title: See what others are listening to
description: "Share what you're listening to with the other people on your server, and see what they're listening to on your Home page."
---

**Listening in the house** shows what other people on your server are listening to: who, which book, and which chapter. It's for people who share one server.

It's **off by default** and works **both ways**. You see others only while you share too, and they see you only while they share.

## Turning it on

1. Open the [Household page](household.md).
2. Under **Listening in the house**, turn on **Share what I'm listening to with the house**.

There's one switch per server. When several people [share a device](profiles.md), each sets their own. Turn it off any time: once the server saves the change, you stop showing up for others, and they disappear from your Home page. Demo accounts can't turn it on.

If you change the switch offline in the web player, it says **Waiting to sync** until the network returns, then **Saving**. Keep the tab open. In the phone apps, changing it needs a connection, and the app tells you if the change didn't go through.

If no switch can show because a server can't be reached, the page says which one and offers **Retry**. With no network, it says you're offline and checks again by itself. If it offers **Reconnect** or names a sign-in proxy, see [Getting back in](connecting.md#getting-back-in-after-signing-out).

## What you see

**On your Home page**, a **Listening in the house** section appears once someone who shares has listened in the last day. Each card shows their name, the book, the chapter when known, and whether they're listening now. A green dot means they're listening right now.

Tap a book you have access to and its page opens. If only one disc of a joined
book was shared with you, you may see a card for that disc. When you can't open
the book, the card shows a plain cover (or a cover already on your device) and
tapping it does nothing.

![Listening in the house on the Home page: one person listening now and one paused, with Sharing settings beside the title](/img/screenshots/web-player/home-house.png)

**On a book's page**, an **In the house** card shows anyone who shares and is on that same book.

![A book's page with the In the house card: one person listening now, and the chapter](/img/screenshots/web-player/book-in-the-house.png)

The cards update about once a minute. Someone whose app [saves once a minute](playback.md#your-position-follows-you) can be a further minute behind.

## Who shows up

- Only people on the same server who share. You never see yourself.
- Only their most recent book, if they listened in the last 24 hours.
- Only listening done while their sharing was on.
- Only listening saved in a library or folder you have access to. If their latest listening was outside those folders, that person doesn't appear.
- Never kids accounts, demo accounts or disabled accounts.

## What is and isn't shared

**Shared** while your switch is on: your username, your most recent book, your place in it, and when you last listened.

**Never shared**: your device, your network address, or which app you use.

The admin can already see everyone's listening in the console's [Activity](../admin/activity.md#what-the-server-records), whether or not you share.
