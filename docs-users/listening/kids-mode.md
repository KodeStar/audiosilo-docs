---
title: Set up kids mode for a child
description: "Give a child a simple AudioSilo with big story covers, a big player and a bedtime, and choose what they can hear."
---

**Kids mode** turns AudioSilo into something a child can use on their own: their stories as big covers, a big simple player, and a bedtime. There's no search, no settings and nothing else to wander into. It works in the iPhone and Android apps and the web player.

## Setting it up

Kids mode belongs to a [profile](profiles.md): a person on this device.

### The quick way: add a kid to your household

On the [Household page](household.md), tap **Add someone to your household** and leave **Kids account** on. The child gets their own account on your server, with no admin needed, and is signed in on this device in kids mode. See [Adding a kid](household.md#adding-a-kid) for the steps and [What they can listen to](household.md#what-they-can-listen-to) for choosing their books.

### Kids mode for any profile

Kids mode also works for a child whose account the server's admin made. Kids mode never shows more than the account can open.

1. Tap **Add someone** on [Who's listening?](profiles.md#adding-someone) and sign in with the child's account.
2. Switch back to yourself.
3. On the [Household page](household.md), under the child's name in **On this device**, turn on **Kids mode**.

### The library and bedtime

Under the child's name on the Household page, with **Kids mode** on, pick:

- **Library** - the one library kids mode shows. For a kid in your household, the collections you share are shown instead.
- **Bedtime** - 7:30 PM unless you change it.

Give the grown-ups on the device a [PIN](profiles.md#a-pin-for-a-person). Without one, a child leaving kids mode can choose a grown-up.

Kids mode can't be turned on for the last grown-up on a device, except on a kid's own device (below).

## What the child sees

![Kids mode on a computer: Hi Leo, the bedtime card, the big player and the story tiles](/img/screenshots/web-player/kids.png)

- **"Hi Leo"** at the top, with the **Grown-ups** button.
- **The bedtime card**, saying when bedtime is.
- **The story playing now**, in a big player with four big buttons: previous chapter, back 30 seconds, play/pause and next chapter. Stories play at normal speed.
- **Your stories** - big cover tiles, part-way stories first. Tap one to play it.

![Kids mode on a phone: the bedtime card, the big player buttons and the story tiles](/img/screenshots/web-player/phone-kids.png)

Kids mode doesn't show podcasts. In the car, [CarPlay or Android Auto](in-the-car.md#a-kids-profile-in-the-car) shows only the child's stories. Without a connection, stories downloaded for the child still play.

## Bedtime

From bedtime until 6:00 AM, starting a story starts a **20-minute sleep timer**. The story then fades out and stops. It works like the [sleep timer](sleep-timer.md), but the child doesn't set anything. It never starts in the car.

## Leaving kids mode

1. **Press and hold Grown-ups for 2 seconds.**
2. On [Who's listening?](profiles.md#whos-listening), choose a grown-up, with their PIN if they have one.

On Android, the back button never leaves kids mode.

To stop using kids mode, turn **Kids mode** off for that person on the [Household page](household.md). This only changes what the device shows, never what the account can hear.

## On a kid's own device

A kid in your household can have their own phone or tablet:

1. On their card on the Household page, tap **Set up their own device**.
2. Scan the code with the AudioSilo app on their device.

The app there starts in kids mode by itself. See [Their devices](household.md#their-devices). A grown-up can hold **Grown-ups** and sign in with their own account to change settings, then remove themselves again.

## Good to know

### If a kid is signed out

The kids screen says to ask a grown-up. Hold **Grown-ups**, choose yourself, open the [Household page](household.md#when-a-member-is-signed-out-on-this-device), and tap **Sign in again** on the child's card.

### Links in kids mode

Kids mode never opens a sign-in link or pairing QR code. It asks a grown-up to open the link after leaving kids mode. The link isn't used up.
