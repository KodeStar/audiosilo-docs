---
title: Add your household
description: "Add your kids and the grown-ups you live with to your AudioSilo server yourself, choose what they can listen to, and manage their devices."
---

A **household member** is a kid or a grown-up you add to your server yourself, from the player. Each gets their own account, with their own place in every book, their own Up next and their own stats. You choose what they can listen to, with no admin, invite or email needed.

To add someone, open the **Household** page and tap **Add someone to your household**.

How this differs from the other ways to share:

- **Inviting someone**: the server's admin invites them, and they get a full account of their own. See [Invite people](../admin/users-and-invites.md).
- **Profiles** let several people use one device. See [Share a device](profiles.md).

## Finding it

- **On a phone**: the **Me** tab, then **Household**.
- **On a tablet or computer**: **Household** under [You](you.md), or in your profile menu (top right).
- In [Settings](settings.md#household), and **Manage household** on [Who's listening?](profiles.md#whos-listening).

![The Household page on a phone, with On this device and the first person's card](/img/screenshots/web-player/phone-household.png)

The page has three parts:

- **On this device** - everyone who listens on this device. See [Share a device](profiles.md#on-the-household-page).
- **Your household** - the people you've added, with **Add someone to your household**.
- **Listening in the house** - whether you share what you're listening to. See [See what others are listening to](in-the-house.md#turning-it-on).

![The Household page at Your household: a kid's card, a grown-up's card, and Add someone to your household](/img/screenshots/web-player/household.png)

## Household members

You manage the people you add. You choose what they can listen to, rename them, sign their devices out, and delete their account. They can't add people of their own, and a member is never an admin.

The section shows how many you've added, out of the most the server allows (6 unless your admin changed it). If you can't add anyone, it says why. Usually the admin turned adding off, or you've reached the limit.

## Adding a kid

1. On the Household page, tap **Add someone to your household**.
2. Type their **Name**.
3. Leave **Kids account** on.
4. Under **What can they listen to?**, tick the collections to share with them. Or tap **Create Evie's books** for a new collection.
5. Tap **Add and sign in here**.

![Adding a kid: the name, Kids account on, the collections to share, and Add and sign in here](/img/screenshots/web-player/household-add-kid.png)

The kid is now signed in on this device in [kids mode](kids-mode.md). You stay the person listening.

If your own profile has no [PIN](profiles.md#a-pin-for-a-person), the app offers to set one. Set one. Without a PIN, a child who leaves kids mode can switch to you.

A kids account has no password. It can't make API keys, pair a device, share collections or join Listening in the house. It signs in only on devices you set up for it.

## Adding a grown-up

1. Tap **Add someone to your household** and type their name.
2. Turn **Kids account** off.
3. Choose what they can listen to.
4. Tap **Add and show their code**.
5. They scan the QR code with the iOS or Android app on their phone, or open the link. It works once, for 10 minutes.

If they'll also listen on your device, tap **They'll also use this device**.

![A grown-up added: the QR code with its link, They'll also use this device and Done](/img/screenshots/web-player/household-add-grownup.png)

## What they can listen to

- **Everything I can listen to** - whatever you can listen to on the server, now and later.
- **Only collections I share** - just the books in the [collections](browsing.md#collections) you share with them. Add a book to one of those collections and they can play it straight away.

They also keep anything the admin gave their account directly. To change it, tap **Change** on their card.

## A member's card

Each person you've added has a card on the Household page. On it you can:

- change **What they can listen to**;
- turn **Kids account** on or off;
- see and sign out [their devices](#their-devices);
- **Rename** them;
- **Delete their account**, or **Remove from this device**.

![A member's card: what they can listen to, the Kids account switch, their devices, Rename, and Delete their account](/img/screenshots/web-player/household-member.png)

## Their devices

The card lists every device the member is signed in on, each with **Sign out**.

- **Set up their own device** shows a code to scan with the app on their phone or tablet. A kid's own device starts in [kids mode](kids-mode.md#on-a-kids-own-device).
- **Sign in on this device** signs them in here too, as a person of their own.

## When a member is signed out on this device

Their card shows **Signed out**, with **Sign in again**. While you're the person listening, tap it to sign them back in with no password. They keep their place, PIN and kids mode.

A kid can't do this from kids mode. Hold **Grown-ups**, choose yourself, and tap **Sign in again** on their card.

## Deleting a member, or removing them from this device

- **Remove from this device** signs them out here and deletes their downloads here. Their account and their place in every book stay on the server.
- **Delete their account** deletes the account from the server, with their history, bookmarks and notes. It can't be undone, so you type their name to confirm.

## Good to know

- **A lost phone.** Signing your phone out from your [account page](account.md#signed-in-devices) signs out the members on it too, unless you untick them.
- **Turned off by the admin.** If the admin turns off a member's account, their devices stop working until it's turned back on.
- **What the admin sees.** Members are real accounts, shown in the admin console with the person who manages them. See [Household members](../admin/users-and-invites.md#household-members).
