The full player is the big view of the book you're listening to. On a phone it opens when you start a book. On a tablet or computer, open it from the [player bar](playback.md#the-mini-player-and-the-player-bar).

![The full player on a computer, with the companion column on the right](/img/screenshots/web-player/player.png)

## What's on it

From top to bottom:

- **The top row**: a **down arrow** to minimise the player (the book keeps playing), the server and series, and the [three-dot menu](#the-three-dot-menu).
- **The cover** and the **chapter title**. Tap the title to see the chapters.
- **The status line**: whether your [place is saved](playback.md#your-position-follows-you), how far through you are, and the [time left](#time-left).
- **The [seek bar](#the-seek-bar-and-the-timeline)** for the chapter, and a timeline of the whole book.
- **The controls**: previous chapter, skip back, play/pause, skip forward, next chapter. Skips are 15 seconds back and 30 forward, unless you change them in [Settings](settings.md#playback).
- **The actions**: [speed](#playback-speed), the [sleep timer](sleep-timer.md), [bookmark](bookmarks-and-notes.md), [output](playback.md#play-to-another-speaker-airplay-and-chromecast), [Cast](chromecast.md) and [Up next](up-next.md).

The [companion](companion.md) sits beside or below the player. It holds the characters, the recaps, the ebook [Text](companion.md#text), the chapters, and your bookmarks, notes and history.

## On a phone, a tablet and a computer

- **On a phone**, the player fills the screen. Chips under the actions (**Who's who**, **Story so far**, **Text** and **Chapters**) open the companion as a sheet.

  ![The player on a phone, with the companion chips under the actions](/img/screenshots/web-player/phone-player.png)

- **On a tablet**, the companion sits under the controls. Scroll down to reach it.
- **On a computer**, the companion is a column on the right. Up next is in the [drawer](up-next.md#opening-up-next) beside the page.

## The seek bar and the timeline

The seek bar covers the **current chapter**, so a small movement is a small jump, even in a long book. Small icons above it mark your bookmarks.

- **Tap** the bar to jump there.
- **Drag** to scrub. A label shows where you'll land.

Under the bar you'll see the time into the chapter, the time left in it, and when it ends by the clock. A book without chapter marks uses its files or its [parts](playback.md#books-without-chapters) instead.

The **whole-book timeline** below shows each chapter as a segment. Pins mark your bookmarks and notes. Tap a pin to jump to it, or tap anywhere else to jump in the book.

Neither bar takes you right to the end: a tap there stops 30 seconds short, so a slip can't finish the book. To finish a book on purpose, use **Mark as Finished**.

## Time left

Wherever AudioSilo shows the time left, it's real time at the speed you listen to that book: *"5h 12m left at 1.25×"*. Each book uses its own speed. A book you haven't started uses your default speed from [Settings](settings.md#playback).

## Undo a jump

![The player bar just after a chapter skip, with the Back to button above it](/img/screenshots/web-player/dock-undo.png)

After any jump of more than a minute, a **Back to 17:26:50** button appears for a few seconds. Tap it to go back. This works for the seek bar, chapter skips and bookmarks, and even for a scrub on the lock screen, headphones or a car.

In the full player the button replaces the status line. On a tablet or computer, it floats just above the player bar, over the play controls.

## The three-dot menu

- **View book details** opens the book's page. The book keeps playing.
- **Chapters** opens the chapter list.
- **View end credits** shows the [end credits](end-of-book.md#the-end-credits) early. It's handy for skipping long spoken credits.
- **Mark as Finished** finishes the book now.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** lists the [keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md), in the web player.

## Playback speed

![The speed sheet on a phone: the slider, the presets with the time left at each, and the Smart speed and Voice boost switches](/img/screenshots/web-player/phone-speed-sheet.png)

Tap the speed (such as `1×`) to change it, from **0.5×** to **2×** in steps of 0.05. Each preset shows how much of the book would be left at that speed. Voices keep their pitch.

Speed is remembered for each book. New books start at your default speed from Settings.

## Smart speed and Voice boost

Two switches in the speed sheet change how the book sounds. They're also in [Settings](settings.md#playback). Both start off, and each applies to every book on this device.

- **Smart speed** shortens the silences between words and sentences, without making the voice faster. It works on **Android** only.
- **Voice boost** evens out quiet and loud voices, so a soft narrator is easier to follow in a noisy car or kitchen. It works on **Android**, **iPhone and iPad**, and in the web player in **Chrome, Edge and Firefox**. It doesn't work in Safari or in any browser on an iPhone or iPad.

On Android, the speed sheet shows how much time Smart speed has saved on this device. A book's page shows what it saved on that book, under [Your listening](book-page.md#about-this-book).
