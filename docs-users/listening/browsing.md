Once you're [connected](connecting.md), AudioSilo gives you a few ways to find something to listen to: the home screen, the Library (by book, author, series, narrator, collection or folder), search, and your own favourites.

## Getting around

AudioSilo arranges itself to fit the screen it's on.

**On a phone** - the iOS and Android apps, or the web player in a narrow window - a tab bar along the bottom has five tabs:

- **Home** - where you are in your book, and your shelves (below).
- **Library** - everything on your server, by book, author, series, narrator, collection or folder.
- **Search** - find books, series, authors, narrators, and characters you've already met.
- **Downloads** - books saved on this device for [offline listening](offline-downloads.md).
- **Me** - [You](you.md): your listening stats, your [Year in listening](year-in-listening.md), your [Journal](journal.md), your [Settings](settings.md), your [household](household.md) and your [account](account.md).

Each tab remembers where you were. Open a book from Search, switch to Library for a moment, come back to Search, and the book is still open there; going back takes you to your search results. Tap the tab you're already on to jump back to its first page.

At the top of each tab's first page, beside the big title, sits the **Up next** button with the number of books in your queue - see [Up next](up-next.md).

**On a tablet or computer** - or any window wide enough - the tabs move to a **top bar** across the top of the screen:

![The web player on a tablet: the top bar with icon-only destinations, the home screen, and the player bar along the bottom](/img/screenshots/web-player/tablet-home.png)

From left to right:

- The **AudioSilo** logo (tap it to go Home), with the server you're using underneath - or, if it can't be reached, a note naming it as **not answering** (*"Hearthside isn't answering"*; **offline** when this device has no network at all), or that it **needs signing in again**. If you've connected more than one server, it says how many more, or names one that **isn't answering**.
- **Home**, **Library**, **Downloads** and **You** (your [listening stats, year and Journal](you.md)). On narrower screens such as a tablet held upright, these show as icons only.
- A **search field**. In the web player it opens [quick search](#quick-search-in-the-web-player); in the tablet app it takes you to the Search page. Where the bar is too narrow for the field (a tablet held upright, say), it shrinks to a search button that does the same.
- The **Up next** button, with the number of books queued. It opens [Up next](up-next.md).
- A **gear** button for [Settings](settings.md). It opens Settings over the page you're on, so going back returns you there.
- Your **profile button** - your initial, plus your name on wider screens. It opens a menu that starts with who's **Listening as**, then the other people on this device (pick one to switch to them; see [Sharing a device (profiles)](profiles.md)) and **Who's listening?**, then your **servers**, each with a line saying who you're signed in as, or that it's **offline** or **needs signing in again** (pick one to open its [account page](account.md)); **Add a server** (in the apps; the web player only reaches its own server); your [Journal](journal.md); [Household](household.md); [Settings](settings.md); **Account on** your main server; and a switch between **light and dark** appearance.

![The profile menu open from the top bar](/img/screenshots/web-player/profile-menu.png)

Under the top bar, a slim row shows the title of the page you're on, or a **Back** button once you've opened something, such as a book. On the Library page that row also carries the Library's sections and its controls (see [The Library](#the-library)). While a book is playing, the [player bar](playback.md#the-mini-player-and-the-player-bar) runs along the bottom of the window.

If you get around the web player with a keyboard, **Tab** moves between controls and **Space** presses the one you're on - buttons, tabs, option choices and switches alike. The player itself has [keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md) too (press **?** to list them).

The browser tab is named after the page you're on: a book, an author, a series, **Library**, **Settings** and so on. While a book is playing, the tab shows ▶ and that book's title instead, wherever you are in the player, so you can find it among your other tabs. Pause it and the tab goes back to naming the page.

:::note
The **Downloads** tab only appears where downloading is possible. In the web player that means a secure (`https://`) address - see [Offline downloads](offline-downloads.md).
:::

## The home screen

The home screen is built around what *you* are doing, not just what's on the server:

![The web player home screen: the greeting, the book you're on with its chapter scale, This week, and the shelves below](/img/screenshots/web-player/home.png)

- **A greeting** with today's date and a small line that tells you, honestly, whether your place is safe: **Progress synced 7 minutes ago** once your servers have it (the time is when this device last checked your progress with them), or **Saved on this device, will sync** while a place is still waiting to sync, or while the server that holds your latest place isn't answering. If you've connected more than one server, it also says how many Home is gathering from, or names one that **isn't answering** (with an amber dot). On a phone, only a server that isn't answering is named, on its own line under the date.
- **Previously on** - when you come back to the book you're on after **12 days or more**, a dark card above the book you're on reminds you where you were: *"Previously on The Way of Kings · 14 days ago"*, the chapter you left it in, and the latest community recap you're already past (so it never gives away what you haven't heard - *"Spoiler-safe: written up to chapter 22."*). **Resume, with 30 seconds of overlap** starts the book half a minute before where you stopped (your latest place, whether this device or another one saved it), at your speed; **Read the full recap** opens the book's Recaps. It appears only for books the community database has recaps for, and not while the book is already loaded in the player. Close it with its **X** and it stays away until you next open the app.
- **The book you're on**, in a card tinted with its cover's colours: the series and book number, the title, **Chapter 6 of 24** with the chapter's name, and a scale of the whole book with a tick for each chapter (and a small pin for each bookmark). Under it: how far through you are, how much is left at the speed you listen to that book, and, once there's enough listening to go on, the day you'll **finish at your pace**. **Resume chapter 6** carries on where you stopped. Offline, or while the book's server isn't answering, a book that isn't downloaded can't start: the button shows **Not downloaded** instead, with a line saying it plays once you're back online (or once the server is answering again, naming it). Home keeps the books' titles offline too. For books the community database knows, **Who's who** (with the number of characters you've met so far) and **Story so far** open the book's Characters and Recaps tabs, which [never show more than you've heard](recaps-and-characters.md#nothing-gets-spoiled-before-you-reach-it).
- **This week** - a ring for the books you've finished this year against your yearly goal (or just how many you've finished, if you haven't set one), the hours you've listened in the last seven days, your listening streak, and a small bar for each of the last seven days.
- **Continue listening** - the other books you've started, most recent first, each showing how much is left. Tap one to open it, or open its [book menu](#the-book-menu) to resume, mark it finished, and more. **All in progress** opens the Library with just those books.
- **Next in your series** - what comes after the books you're on, or just finished, with a line saying why ("After The Way of Kings, which you're 40% into"). It includes the next book even when you don't have it yet: those show as a pale "ghost" cover marked **Not in your library**, and tapping one opens the series page.
- **Coming up in your series** - books you can preorder, books just released and the volumes you're missing in the series you have, each with its date (*"Out Nov 12, 2026"*). It only shows when there's something in it; **See all** opens the Upcoming page. See [Upcoming releases](upcoming.md).
- **New episodes** - recent episodes from the podcasts you follow, newest first, when your server has a podcast library. An episode you're listening to shows in the card for what you're on and in Continue listening, but never in the book shelves (Next in your series, the smart shelves, Recently finished). See [Podcasts](podcasts.md#new-episodes-on-home).
- **Listening in the house** - when you and others on your server share what you're listening to, what they're on right now or listened to in the last 24 hours. It's off by default; see [Listening in the house](in-the-house.md).
- **Smart shelves** - saved filters that keep themselves up to date: **Finish what you started**, **Short listens** (under 5 hours), **"Name" reads** for the narrator you've listened to most lately, and **Added this week**. Tap one to open the Library with that filter on (the narrator's shelf opens their page). They appear once there are at least two worth showing.
- **Recently added**, **Favourites** (once you have some) and **Recently finished**, each with a **See all** link.

:::tip
If you're connected to more than one server, Home combines all of them. A book that lives on one of your other servers carries a small flag with that server's name.
:::

In the web player, Home and the Library open straight away with what they showed on your last visit, then quietly update from the server a moment later. The Library shows its first books first and fills in the rest as they arrive.

## The Library

The **Library** tab is everything on your server, sorted the way you want to look at it. Across the top are its sections: **Books**, **Authors**, **Series**, **Narrators**, **Collections** and **Folders**. On a tablet or computer they sit in the slim row under the top bar, with the page's controls at its right-hand end; on a phone they're a row you can swipe sideways, under the big **Library** title.

![The Library's Books section: the sections and controls along the top, and the cover grid with progress bars](/img/screenshots/web-player/library.png)

A **podcast library** has just two sections, **Shows** and **Folders**: see [Podcasts](podcasts.md#shows-in-the-library). It appears in the app from version 2.4.0, with a microphone beside its name in the picker.

Every section except Folders shows **one library at a time**. If you have more than one library (or more than one server), a **Library** picker sits with the controls, with your libraries grouped under each server's name; with a single library there's nothing to pick, so it doesn't appear. Your choice is remembered on that device.

If the web player is offline before your libraries have loaded, the Library and Folders say **You're offline**: your libraries show once a server answers, and books you've downloaded are in **Downloads**. If the Library has no library to show because a server didn't answer, it says "Could not load libraries." with **Retry**. It fills in by itself once the server is back. If your sign-in to that server ended, it says **Sign in again to &lt;server&gt;** with **Reconnect** instead.

### Books

Every book in the library, as a cover grid or a list:

- **Filters** along the top: **Not started**, **In progress** and **Finished** (each with a count of how many books it would show), **Downloaded** (on this device), and a length: **Under 5 h**, **5 to 15 h** or **Over 15 h**. **Clear** takes them all off. Once a filter is on, **Clear** moves to the front of the row, followed by any genre or tag you chose, so both stay in view on a narrow screen.
- **Genre** and **Tag**, after the other filters, when a book in the library has one. Each opens a list of the library's genres (or tags) with how many books each would show, plus **Any genre** (**Any tag**) to drop it; the chip then names your pick (*"Fantasy"*). Capitals don't matter: "Sci-Fi" and "sci-fi" are one tag. Tapping a genre or tag on a [book's page](book-page.md#about-this-book) opens this view with it chosen.
- **Sort** by **Recently added**, **Title**, **Author** or **Length**. Sorted by title, the books are grouped under letter headings, with an **A to Z** rail for jumping straight to a letter. A leading "The", "A" or "An" is skipped and accented letters file under their plain letter (*Émile* under E), and a book with no title is shown, and sorted, by its folder's name.
- **Grid or list**. Your choice is remembered on that device, and the same choice applies to collections and Favourites. On a tablet or computer the list has columns for the narrator, the length and your progress.

![The Genre filter open over Library > Books: each genre with how many books it would show, and Any genre](/img/screenshots/web-player/library-genre.png)

A big library loads in the background, page by page, while you browse; the filters and sorting work on the whole of it once it's in.

A cover with no art shows the book's initials instead.

#### The book menu

Every book has a menu of things you can do with it: a drop-down on a tablet or computer, a sheet from the bottom on a phone. In the **list**, it's the book's **...** button. On a **cover** anywhere in the app (Home's shelves, the Library grid, author and series pages, search results, Favourites), open it by:

- pressing and holding the cover;
- right-clicking it, in the web player;
- moving to it with the keyboard and pressing the **Menu** key (or **Shift+F10**);
- with a screen reader, choosing the cover's **More actions**.

The pale "ghost" covers of books you don't have have no menu. The menu holds:

- **Play** or **Resume**.
- **Add to Up next** or **Remove from Up next** (see [Up next](up-next.md)).
- **Add to collection...** (see [Collections](#collections)).
- **Download for offline**, **Cancel download** or **Remove download** (which asks first).
- **Mark as finished**, or **Mark as not finished** for a book you've finished. Both show a short note with **Undo** (on an older server, Mark as finished shows the note without Undo).
- **More in this series**, for a book in a series.

### Authors and narrators

Each author or narrator gets a card with how many of their books you have, how many hours that adds up to, and a fan of a few of their covers. Long lists get letter headings. If some books don't name an author (or narrator), a line at the end says how many.

Tap a card to open that person's page:

- Their name, with tiles for the books here, the total length, and how many you've finished or listened to.
- A **shelf for each series** of theirs. A book in more than one series sits on each of those shelves, at its number there. Tap a series name to open its page.
- Their **other books** in a grid.
- **Read by** chips on an author's page (the narrators of their books), or **Books by** chips on a narrator's page.

![An author's page: the stat tiles, a series shelf, and their other books](/img/screenshots/web-player/author.png)

### Series

Each series gets a card with a small shelf of the books you have, gaps where a number is missing, and a line such as "4 of 6 · 1 in progress".

![The Library's Series section: one card per series, each with a mini shelf of its books](/img/screenshots/web-player/library-series.png)

Tap a card to open the [series page](#series-pages).

A book can be in more than one series (*Guards! Guards!* is Discworld book 8 and the first City Watch book). It then appears in each, at its number there. This needs a recent server; an older one shows each book in its main series only.

### Collections

Collections are lists of books you put together yourself: "Road trip", "For the kids", "Book club". The **Collections** section shows:

- **Favourites**, the books you've hearted on every server.
- **Your collections**, and any that someone else on your server has **shared with you**. A card says who's involved: "Shared with sam" on one of yours, "Shared by sam" on someone else's.
- **New collection**, to make one: a name and, if you like, a description.

Collections belong to a server rather than to one library, so in this section the picker chooses a **server**.

To add a book, open its [book menu](#the-book-menu) and choose **Add to collection...**, or use **Add to collection** on its [page](book-page.md#listening-and-the-buttons). The dialog lists your collections, so you can add the book to several at once, take it out of one, or **Create and add** a new one there and then. A collection with nothing in it yet says *"Nothing in this collection yet"* and reminds you of the same two ways in.

![A collection's page: its name, description and total length, the owner's buttons, and its books](/img/screenshots/web-player/collection.png)

A collection's page shows its name, description, how many books it holds and their total length. On **your own** collections you can:

- **Edit** the name and description.
- **Share** it with people on the same server. They can see it but not change it, and each person only sees the books they're allowed to open.
- **Delete** it. The books stay in your library; anyone it's shared with loses it too.
- In the list view, use each book's menu to **Move up**, **Move down**, or **Remove from collection** (with **Undo**).

On a collection **someone shared with you**, there's a single **Leave** button instead. Leaving takes it out of your collections; its owner can share it with you again.

:::note
Sharing isn't offered on a demo account.
:::

### Folders

**Folders** is the folder-by-folder view: your libraries (with a **Favourites** row at the top), and inside each one its **folders and books**, mirroring exactly how the audiobooks are organized on the server (typically author folders containing book folders).

- **Folder rows** open a folder; **book rows** open a book. Book rows show the duration and bitrate.
- Folders come first, then files, each in the order a book plays them (`2` before `10`). An `.mka` file with the same name as another audio file beside it (`Book.m4b` and `Book.mka`) is a copy of it, so it isn't listed, just as it isn't part of the book.
- A book your server **hasn't scanned yet** (just copied in, before the next scan reaches it) still shows as a book, captioned **"Not scanned yet"**. Tap it and its page opens as a book: the server reads it there and then (a big book can take a moment), and you can play it at once; back in the folder, the row then shows the book like any other. Favouriting one with its heart adds it to the library too, so it shows on your Favourites like any book. On an older server such a folder opens as a folder instead, listing its files: tap one to open the book.
- **Breadcrumbs** at the top show where you are and jump back up any level.
- Big folders (more than a couple dozen entries) get a **filter box** ("Filter this folder…") and an **A–Z rail** on the right edge for jumping straight to a letter - a library with thousands of authors stays quick to navigate.
- Your scroll position is remembered, so backing out of a book returns you to where you were.

With more than one server, each server's libraries sit under its name. If a server doesn't answer, its group says "Couldn't load libraries from &lt;server&gt;." with **Retry**, which asks just that server again. Your other servers' libraries stay where they are. If your sign-in to a server ended, Folders says **Sign in again to &lt;server&gt;** with **Reconnect** instead. Inside a folder it's the same: a folder on a server that needs signing in again shows **Sign in again to &lt;server&gt;** with **Reconnect**, not an error with **Retry**.

What you see here is what your account has been granted - see [Sharing](../admin/sharing.md) if some of the server's content isn't visible to you.

### When your server is older

The Library adapts to what your server can do. Authors, Series and Narrators (and the author, narrator and series pages) need a recent AudioSilo server, and so do Collections, Up next, the This week card, Next in your series, **Mark as not finished**, [Listening in the house](in-the-house.md) and the [Cast](chromecast.md) button. On an older server those parts simply don't appear, and everything else works as before. Ask whoever runs your server to update it to get them.

## Series pages

A series page is the series laid out like a bookcase:

![A series page: the hero with its progress track, the bookcase of spines with the current book face-out, and the list of every book](/img/screenshots/web-player/series.png)

- **At the top**, washed in the colours of its covers: "Series · " and the author, the series name, and how many books it has, how many are on your servers, how many aren't in your library, and how many hours of listening it adds up to.
- **A progress track** for the whole series - finished books in green, the one you're partway through in pink, missing ones hatched - with a line such as "40% into book 2 · 6h 23m of listening ahead".
- **A reading-order switch** - **Publication**, **Chronological** or **Recommended** - for series the community database knows more than one order for. Your choice is remembered per series on that device, and the [book page's Series tab](recaps-and-characters.md#series-with-more-than-one-reading-order) uses the same choice.
- **The bookcase**: a spine for each book in reading order, wider for longer books. The book you've chosen is turned face-out, and the one you're listening to wears a pink ribbon. Books you don't have stand as pale "ghost" spines with their real titles, and a book that's on another of your servers is tinted to say so. A ghost that isn't out yet says when it will be (**Out Nov 12, 2026**; see [Upcoming releases](upcoming.md#on-series-pages)).
- **Under the shelf**, for the chosen book: its number, year and length, where you are with it, and **one** thing to do: **Resume chapter 3** for the book you're on, **Queue it** for one you haven't started (it goes on [Up next](up-next.md); on a server without Up next this is **Play**), **Open** for one you've finished, **Listen on** (or **Open on**) another of your servers for a copy that lives there, or **View on AudioSilo Meta** for a book you don't have. A small legend explains the colours.
- **Every book in the series** listed below, each with the same action, and a credit line for the community series data.
- **Keep ahead offline**, on devices that can download: a shortcut to the [Keep the next books ready](offline-downloads.md#keep-the-next-books-ready) setting (Off, 1, 2 or 3). It's the same setting as on the Downloads page, so it applies to every series, with your Up next queue first.

The full bookcase, with ghost spines and reading orders, comes from the [community metadata database](../community/meta-site.md) and needs your server to have the lookup switched on. Without it, the page shows the books you have in that series, with a dashed **Book 3** placeholder wherever your copies skip a number.

An omnibus your server numbers as a range (books **1-3**, say; see [Numbering an omnibus](../admin/books.md#numbering-an-omnibus-or-box-set)) shows as `#1-3` and fills the places of the books inside it, so they don't show as missing. An older app shows it as book 1 instead.

## A book's page

Tap a book anywhere to open its page: where you are in it, **Resume** (or **Start listening**), the download, Up next, favourite and collection buttons, About and other versions, and tabs for the chapters, your bookmarks, history and notes, the book's files, and the community's recaps, characters and series. It has a page of its own: [A book's page](book-page.md).

## Search

The **Search** tab finds **books, series, authors, narrators, and characters you've already met**, across every library you have access to - and across every server you've added. (On a tablet app, the search field in the top bar takes you here; in the web player it opens [quick search](#quick-search-in-the-web-player) instead.)

Before you type, the page shows your **Recent searches** (with **Clear**) and **Browse** cards for the selected library's Authors, Series, Narrators and Collections, each with a count; tap one to open that Library section.

![Search results for "holmes", grouped into Books and Series](/img/screenshots/web-player/search.png)

Results appear as you type, in groups:

- **Books** - matching titles, authors, narrators and series (and, on a recent server, genres and tags) from every server. A book you have on more than one server shows once, with "Also on" naming the others, and a book that lives on another of your servers carries a small flag with that server's name.
- **Series** - matching series, each shown as its card with a small shelf of the books you have, opening its [series page](#series-pages).
- **People** - matching authors and narrators, each tagged with their role, opening their page.
- **Characters** - characters from books you've started whose names (or other names they go by) match, opening the book they're from.

A group with more than a few results has a **Show all** button. If one group can't be searched just now (a server didn't answer, say), it says so with its own **Retry**, and the other groups carry on. If a server is offline, a line names it so you know its books aren't in the results.

Tap a result to open it; going back returns you to the same results. Switch to another tab and your search is cleared, so the next one starts fresh. A search that led somewhere is kept as a recent search on that device (the last five).

### Characters only once you've met them

Character search is built so it can't spoil anything. It only looks at books you've **started or finished** (your eight most recent), and it only names a character you've **already reached** in that book, using the same rule as the [Characters tab](recaps-and-characters.md#nothing-gets-spoiled-before-you-reach-it). Characters who appear later aren't named at all - not even under another name they go by. You'll only see a quiet count, such as "2 more matches after your place in the book, hidden to avoid spoilers", so you know the search didn't simply miss them.

Character results need your server's community metadata lookup, and books the community has written character cards for.

### Quick search in the web player

In the web player on a tablet or computer, clicking the search field in the top bar opens **quick search**: one box for finding a book *or* doing something, without leaving the page you're on. You can also open it from the keyboard, anywhere in the app: **⌘K** on a Mac (**Ctrl+K** elsewhere), or just **/** when you aren't typing in a box.

![Quick search open over the home screen, with "holmes" typed and the matching books and series listed](/img/screenshots/web-player/palette.png)

Start typing and the results are grouped:

- **Actions** - things you can do right now. While a book is loaded: **Pause** (or **Resume** the current chapter), **Sleep in 30 minutes**, **Sleep at end of chapter** (for books with chapters) and **Open the full player**. On a server with [Up next](up-next.md): **Open Up next**, with how many books are queued. Always: a switch to **light or dark** appearance. Setting a sleep timer this way shows a short confirmation at the bottom of the screen.
- **Books** - matching titles, authors and series from every server you're connected to, the same search as the Search page. Before you type anything, this group is **Continue listening** instead: the books you're partway through, with how much you've listened to.
- **Series**, **Authors**, **Narrators** and **Characters** - up to three of each, from the same search as the Search page. Characters follow the same rule: only ones you've met, with a quiet note counting any you haven't.
- **Go to** - Home, Library and Downloads, then [Your listening](you.md#your-listening), [Year in listening](year-in-listening.md), your [Journal](journal.md), [Settings](settings.md) and [Household](household.md).

Use the **arrow keys** to move through the results, **Enter** to open one, and **Esc** to close. Book results say which server and library each one comes from, as on the Search page. Your recent searches appear as **Recent** shortcuts the next time you open it; it's the same list as the Search page's, kept on this device only.

:::note
Book results come from book metadata (title, author, series). To hunt by folder name inside one folder, use the Folders view's filter box instead.
:::

## Favourites

Favourites are your personal shelf - mark anything you want to find again quickly:

- Tap the **heart** on a [book's page](book-page.md), or the heart on any row in the Folders view. You can favourite whole **folders** as well as books (handy for a series or an author). Hearting a book marked "Not scanned yet" reads it there and then, so it is saved as a book, not a folder (if the server can't read it at that moment, it shows as a folder until the next scan reaches it).
- Find them again on the **Favourites** shelf on Home, the **Favourites** card in the Library's Collections section, or the **Favourites** row at the top of the Folders view. Each opens the full list, with books as a cover grid.
- Tap the heart again to remove a favourite.

Favourites are tied to your account, so they follow you across all your devices.
