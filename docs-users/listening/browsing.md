title: Browse and search your library
description: Find something to listen to from Home, the Library, Search and your favourites, on a phone, tablet or computer.

Once you're [connected](connecting.md), there are four ways to find something to listen to: **Home**, the **Library**, **Search** and your **Favourites**. Tap any book to open its [book page](book-page.md), where you can start it.

## Getting around

The layout fits the screen you're on.

**On a phone** (the iOS and Android apps, or the web player in a narrow window), a tab bar along the bottom has five tabs:

- **Home** - the book you're on, and your shelves.
- **Library** - everything on your server.
- **Search** - find books, series, people and characters.
- **Downloads** - books saved on this device for [offline listening](offline-downloads.md).
- **Me** - [You](you.md): your stats, [Journal](journal.md), [Settings](settings.md), [household](household.md) and [account](account.md).

Each tab remembers where you were. Tap the tab you're already on to go back to its first page. The **Up next** button beside each tab's title opens [Up next](up-next.md).

**On a tablet or computer**, the tabs move to a bar across the top:

![The web player on a tablet: the top bar, the home screen and the player bar](/img/screenshots/web-player/tablet-home.png)

From left to right:

- The **AudioSilo** logo, which goes Home. Under it is the server you're using, with a warning if it isn't answering or needs you to sign in again.
- **Home**, **Library**, **Downloads** and **You**. On narrower screens these are icons only.
- A **search field**. In the web player it opens [quick search](#quick-search-in-the-web-player). In the app on a tablet it opens the Search page.
- **Up next**, with the number of books queued.
- A **gear** for [Settings](settings.md).
- Your **profile button**. Its menu switches between [people on this device](profiles.md), lists your servers, and links to your Journal, Household, Settings and account. It also switches between light and dark.

![The profile menu open from the top bar](/img/screenshots/web-player/profile-menu.png)

Under the top bar, a slim row shows the page title, or **Back** once you've opened something. While a book plays, the [player bar](playback.md#the-mini-player-and-the-player-bar) runs along the bottom.

In the web player, **Tab** moves between controls and **Space** presses the one you're on. The web player also has [keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md). Press **?** to list them.

:::note
The **Downloads** tab only appears where downloading is possible. In the web player that needs a secure (`https://`) address.
:::

## The home screen

![The home screen: the greeting, the book you're on, This week and the shelves](/img/screenshots/web-player/home.png)

From the top, Home shows:

- **A greeting** with the date and whether your progress has synced. If listening is still waiting on this device, it says how much (see [Listening on more than one device](playback.md#listening-on-more-than-one-device)).
- **Previously on** - for books with recaps in the community database. When you come back to a book after 12 days or more, a card reminds you where you were, with the latest recap you've already passed. **Resume, with 30 seconds of overlap** starts half a minute before where you stopped.
- **The book you're on** - the chapter, a scale of the whole book, the time left at your speed, and when you'll finish at your pace. **Resume** carries on. **Who's who** and **Story so far** open its characters and recaps, which [never show more than you've heard](recaps-and-characters.md#nothing-gets-spoiled-before-you-reach-it).
- **This week** - books finished this year against your goal, hours listened this week, and your streak.
- **Continue listening** - the other books you've started, most recent first.
- **Next in your series** - what comes after the books you're on. A book you don't have shows as a pale "ghost" cover marked **Not in your library**.
- **Coming up in your series** - preorders, new releases and missing volumes, with dates. See [Upcoming releases](upcoming.md).
- **New episodes** - from podcasts you follow. See [Podcasts](podcasts.md#new-episodes-on-home).
- **Listening in the house** - what others on your server are listening to, when you both share it. See [See what others are listening to](in-the-house.md).
- **Smart shelves** - filters that keep themselves up to date, such as **Finish what you started**, **Short listens** and **Added this week**.
- **Recently added**, **Favourites** and **Recently finished**, each with **See all**.

:::tip
Connected to more than one server? Home combines them. A book from another server carries a small flag with its name.
:::

## The Library

The **Library** tab holds everything on your server. Its sections are **Books**, **Authors**, **Series**, **Narrators**, **Collections** and **Folders**. On a phone, swipe the row of sections sideways.

![The Library's Books section: the sections and controls along the top, and the cover grid](/img/screenshots/web-player/library.png)

If you have more than one library or server, use the **Library** picker beside the controls. Every section except Folders shows one library at a time. A [podcast library](podcasts.md#shows-in-the-library) has just **Shows** and **Folders**.

With more than one server, the picker names each library with its server, like **Books · Home**. Until you choose a library, the Library shows your first server.

### Books

Every book in the library, as a cover grid or a list:

- **Filters**: **Not started**, **In progress**, **Finished**, **Downloaded**, and a length (**Under 5 h**, **5 to 15 h**, **Over 15 h**). **Clear** removes them.
- **Genre** and **Tag**, when the library's books have them. Tapping a genre or tag on a [book's page](book-page.md#about-this-book) opens this view filtered to it.
- **Sort** by **Recently added**, **Title**, **Author** or **Length**. Sorted by title, an **A to Z** rail jumps to a letter.
- **Grid or list**. On a tablet or computer, the list adds columns for narrator, length and progress.

![The Genre filter open over the Library's books](/img/screenshots/web-player/library-genre.png)

#### The book menu

Every book has a menu of actions. To open it:

- tap **...** on a book in a list;
- press and hold a cover, or right-click it in the web player;
- with a keyboard, press the **Menu** key or **Shift+F10** on a cover. A screen reader offers it as **More actions**.

The menu holds:

- **Play** or **Resume**.
- **Add to Up next** or **Remove from Up next**.
- **Add to collection...**
- **Download for offline**, or **Remove download**.
- **Send to watch**, in the iPhone app with a paired [Apple Watch](apple-watch.md#from-your-iphone-send-to-watch).
- **Mark as finished** or **Mark as not finished**, with **Undo**.
- **More in this series**.

### Authors and narrators

Each person gets a card showing how many of their books you have and their total length. Tap a card to open their page: a shelf for each of their series, their other books, and the narrators who read them (or, for a narrator, the authors).

![An author's page: the stat tiles, a series shelf and their other books](/img/screenshots/web-player/author.png)

### Series

Each series gets a card with a small shelf of your books and a line such as "4 of 6 · 1 in progress". Gaps show where a number is missing. Tap a card to open its [series page](#series-pages).

![The Library's Series section, one card per series](/img/screenshots/web-player/library-series.png)

A book in more than one series appears in each. *Guards! Guards!*, for example, is Discworld book 8 and City Watch book 1.

### Collections

Collections are lists of books you make yourself, such as "Road trip" or "Book club". This section shows your **Favourites**, your own collections, any **shared with you**, and **New collection**. Collections belong to a server, not a library, so here the picker chooses a server.

To add a book, choose **Add to collection** from its [book menu](#the-book-menu) or its [book page](book-page.md#listening-and-the-buttons). You can add it to several collections at once, or create a new one there.

![A collection's page: its name, description, the owner's buttons and its books](/img/screenshots/web-player/collection.png)

On your own collection you can:

- **Edit** its name and description.
- **Share** it with people on the same server. They can see it but not change it.
- **Delete** it. The books stay in your library.
- Reorder or remove books, from each book's menu in the list view.

On a collection someone shared with you, **Leave** removes it from your list.

### Folders

**Folders** shows your libraries exactly as they're organized on the server, usually author folders holding book folders.

- Tap a folder to open it, or a book to open its page.
- **Breadcrumbs** at the top take you back up.
- Big folders get a **filter box** and an **A-Z rail**.
- A book the server hasn't scanned yet shows as **Not scanned yet**. You can still open and play it, because the server reads it on the spot.

You see only what an admin has shared with your account. If something is missing, see [Limit what someone can see](../admin/sharing.md).

## Series pages

A series page lays the series out like a bookcase.

![A series page: the progress track, the bookcase of spines and the list of every book](/img/screenshots/web-player/series.png)

- **At the top**: the series name, the author, how many books it has and how many you have.
- **A progress track**: finished books in green, the one you're on in pink, missing ones hatched.
- **A reading-order switch** (**Publication**, **Chronological** or **Recommended**), for series with more than one order. Your choice is remembered per series on that device. The [book page's Series tab](recaps-and-characters.md#series-with-more-than-one-reading-order) shares your choice.
- **The bookcase**: a spine for each book, in order. Books you don't have are pale "ghost" spines. One that isn't out yet shows its [release date](upcoming.md#on-series-pages).
- **Under the shelf**: the chosen book and one action, such as **Resume**, **Queue it** (adds it to [Up next](up-next.md)), or **View on AudioSilo Meta** for a book you don't have.
- **Every book in the series**, listed below.
- **Keep ahead offline**, a shortcut to [Keep the next books ready](offline-downloads.md#keep-the-next-books-ready).

The ghost spines and reading orders come from the community database, [AudioSilo Meta](../community/meta-site.md), so your server needs community metadata switched on. Without it, the page shows only the books you have.

An omnibus numbered as a range, such as books 1-3, fills the places of the books inside it (see [Numbering an omnibus](../admin/books.md#numbering-an-omnibus-or-box-set)).

## Search

The **Search** tab finds books, series, authors, narrators and characters, across every library and server you have. Before you type, it shows your recent searches and cards to browse Authors, Series, Narrators and Collections.

![Search results for "holmes", grouped into Books and Series](/img/screenshots/web-player/search.png)

Results appear as you type, grouped into:

- **Books** - matching titles, authors, narrators, series, genres and tags. A book on more than one server shows once. A Chinese or Japanese title is found by any character or word in it.
- **Series** - each opens its [series page](#series-pages).
- **People** - authors and narrators.
- **Characters** - from books you've started.

Tap a result to open it. Going back returns you to the same results.

### Characters only once you've met them

Character search can't spoil anything. It only looks at books you've started or finished, and only names characters you've already reached. It uses the [same rule as the Characters tab](recaps-and-characters.md#nothing-gets-spoiled-before-you-reach-it). A quiet line counts any matches it hid. Character search needs community metadata switched on for your server.

### Quick search in the web player

In the web player on a tablet or computer, the search field in the top bar opens **quick search**. It finds a book or does something, without leaving the page. Open it from anywhere with **⌘K** on a Mac, **Ctrl+K** elsewhere, or **/**.

![Quick search open over the home screen, with matching books and series](/img/screenshots/web-player/palette.png)

Results are grouped:

- **Actions**, such as **Pause**, **Sleep in 30 minutes**, **Open the full player**, **Open Up next**, or switching light and dark.
- **Books** - the same search as the Search page. Before you type, this shows the books you're partway through.
- **Series**, **Authors**, **Narrators** and **Characters**.
- **Go to** - Home, Library, Downloads, your listening, Journal, Settings and Household.

Use the **arrow keys** to move, **Enter** to open and **Esc** to close.

## Favourites

Favourites are your personal shelf:

- Tap the **heart** on a [book's page](book-page.md), or on any row in Folders. You can favourite a whole folder too, such as an author or a series.
- Find them on Home's **Favourites** shelf, in the Library's **Collections** section, or at the top of **Folders**.
- Tap the heart again to remove one.

Favourites belong to your account, so they follow you to every device.

## Good to know

- **Older servers.** Authors, Series, Narrators, Collections, Up next, This week, Next in your series, **Mark as not finished**, Listening in the house and the Cast button need a recent server. On an older one they don't appear. Ask whoever runs your server to update it.
- **A server that doesn't answer.** The Library and Folders say so, with **Retry**. If your sign-in ended, they offer **Reconnect** instead. Your other servers keep working.
- **More than one server.** The Library stays on the library you chose, even while its server loads or doesn't answer. It never shows another server's books in its place. To browse another server meanwhile, choose one of its libraries in the picker.
- **No connection.** Pages show **You're offline** and load again by themselves. See [With no connection](offline-downloads.md#with-no-connection).
- **Sharing a collection** isn't offered on a demo account.
