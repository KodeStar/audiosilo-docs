The **companion** sits beside the [full player](playback.md#the-full-player) and keeps everything about the book you're listening to one tap away, without leaving the player:

- **Who's who** - the people you've met so far.
- **Story so far** - what's happened up to where you are.
- **Chapters** - every chapter, and how long until each one.
- **Bookmarks**, **Notes** and **History** - your own bookmarks, notes and listening sessions for the book.

Where it is depends on the screen: a column down the right of the full player on a **computer**, under the player's controls on a **tablet** (scroll down), and a sheet on a **phone**, opened from the **Who's who**, **Story so far** and **Chapters** chips under the controls. It reopens on the tab you used last.

Who's who and Story so far come from the community database, [AudioSilo Meta](../community/meta-site.md), so they appear only when your server has community metadata switched on (otherwise the companion starts at Chapters). For a book the database doesn't know, or one nobody has written character notes or recaps for yet, the tab says so.

## Nothing ahead of where you are

Who's who and Story so far follow **where you are right now** - your place as you listen, never behind your saved place - and they move on with you as you play, chapter by chapter. They follow the same rule as the book page's Characters and Recaps tabs (see [Nothing gets spoiled before you reach it](browsing.md#nothing-gets-spoiled-before-you-reach-it)):

- A character you haven't met yet isn't named, and a part of the story isn't shown until you've finished the chapter it covers. A line counts what's held back (*"3 characters you haven't met yet are hidden"*), with **Show anyway** if you want them regardless. Anything shown that way carries a **Spoiler** mark, and **Hide them again** puts it back.
- **Show anyway** counts for both tabs of that book, until you next start the app. Another book starts hidden again.
- Once you've finished a book, everything is shown.

## Who's who

The people you've met, the most recently met first, with how many there are (*"12 people you've met"*). Each card shows a coloured initial, the name and role, any other names they go by, and when they first appear (*"First appears in chapter 7"*, or *"There from the start"*).

A character's description is written for the whole book, so it can give away later events. It stays closed until you **tap the card** to read it, one card at a time.

### Meeting someone new

When you play on into a new chapter and it brings in people you hadn't met, they appear at the top of Who's who with a pink outline and **Just met**, and a note says so wherever you are in the app: *"New in Who's who: Teft, Rock"*, *"You just met them in chapter 12. Nothing past this point is shown."* Tap **Show** to open the player on Who's who.

The note only comes when the book itself carries you into the chapter while it plays. Jumping ahead, skipping, or picking the book up again never brings it up, and nobody is announced twice in one session, even if you go back and play through a chapter again.

## Story so far

The community recaps written to stop where you are, in story order, under a heading such as *"Up to chapter 22"* and the promise *"Written to stop where you are. Nothing after chapter 22 is shown."* A new part appears as you finish each chapter. Until you've finished the first chapter a recap covers, it says *Nothing to recap yet*.

A recap from before the book starts (a catch-up from earlier in the series) is labelled as such. A whole-book summary includes the ending, so while you're still listening it stays behind a row marked **Spoiler**, as on the book page.

## Chapters

Every chapter (or file, for a book without chapters), with the one you're in highlighted and how much of it is left. Chapters you've finished are ticked, and each later one says how long until it starts (*"in 2h 4m"*, at your speed). Tap a chapter to jump to it. The list opens on the chapter you're in.

## Bookmarks, Notes and History

The same lists as on the book's page, for the book that's playing:

- **Bookmarks** - **Add bookmark at 1:16:19** pins where you are; tap a bookmark to jump to it.
- **Notes** - your notes on the book.
- **History** - your listening sessions, by chapter; tap one to go back to where it ended.

A jump from these lists is an ordinary jump in the player, so the [undo button](playback.md#undo-a-jump) offers the way back.

## Who wrote it

Under Who's who and Story so far, a line credits the people who wrote them: *"Community notes from"* the contributors, and the licence they're shared under (tap it to read the licence). Community notes are written by listeners and shared under an open licence, which asks for that credit wherever they appear.
