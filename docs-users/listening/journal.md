The **Journal** is your listening, looked back on. It has a **Diary** of every day you listened, and every **bookmark** and **note** you've made, from every book on every server you use.

## Opening it

The Journal is part of [You](you.md#finding-you):

- **On a phone**: the **Journal** section of the **Me** tab.
- **On a tablet or computer**: **Journal** under **You** in the top bar, or in your [profile menu](browsing.md#getting-around).
- **In the web player's** [quick search](browsing.md#quick-search-in-the-web-player): **Journal**, under Go to.
- **From a book**: **See all in your journal** on its **Bookmarks** or **Notes** tab.

It has three tabs: **Diary**, **Bookmarks** and **Notes**.

## The Diary

![The Journal's Diary: today's card with its 24 hour bar and listening sessions](/img/screenshots/web-player/journal.png)

There's one card for each day you listened, newest first. Each card says how long you listened and shows:

- **A 24 hour bar**, with each stretch of listening drawn where it happened in the day. You can see at a glance whether it was your commute or a late night.
- **The sessions**: the book, when you started, how long you listened, and where you went in the book.

Tap a cover to open that book's [History tab](book-page.md#history).

### Drift-offs

When the [sleep timer](sleep-timer.md#if-you-fell-asleep) stopped a book you'd fallen asleep to, a strip under that session says so.

On the device you were listening on, it offers **Jump back** for a day and a half, or until you play the book again. That takes you to the last moment you touched the player, when you were surely still awake. Elsewhere, **Play from where you drifted off** starts the book where the timer stopped it.

## Bookmarks and notes

![The Journal's Bookmarks tab: the search box, the label chips and bookmarks from several books](/img/screenshots/web-player/journal-bookmarks.png)

The **Bookmarks** and **Notes** tabs gather every one you've made, newest first.

- Tap a cover to open the book, or a time to jump there.
- **Search** looks through book titles, authors and your own words.
- On **Bookmarks**, the **label chips** narrow the list to one kind, such as **Quote** or **Fell asleep**.
- **Edit** and **Delete** work as on a book's page. See [Add bookmarks and notes](bookmarks-and-notes.md).

These two tabs need a recent server. An older server's bookmarks stay on each book's page.

## Exporting your bookmarks and notes

You can export every bookmark and note as a file to keep, print or open in a spreadsheet. The Diary isn't included.

- **In the web player**: **Copy as Markdown**, or **Download** as Markdown or CSV.
- **In the iOS and Android apps**: **Export**, then **Share as Markdown** or **Share as CSV**. Your phone's share sheet opens, so you can save the file or send it to another app.

Markdown groups everything by book, in book order. CSV has one row per entry, and opens in Excel with accents intact.
