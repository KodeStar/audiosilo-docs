---
title: Share a device
description: "Let several people listen on one phone, tablet or computer, each with their own place in every book, and switch between them."
---

When more than one person listens on the same device, each can have a **profile**: a person on this device with their own AudioSilo account. Each keeps their own place in every book, their own [Up next](up-next.md), [bookmarks](bookmarks-and-notes.md) and [stats](you.md). The app shows one person's books at a time.

To add someone, tap **Add someone** on **Who's listening?**. To switch, tap their name there.

## Who's listening?

**Who's listening?** shows everyone on this device. The person listening now has a pink ring. Tap someone to switch to them.

![Who's listening? on a computer: three people, Add someone, and Manage household](/img/screenshots/web-player/profiles.png)

Open it from:

- **on a phone**, the **Me** tab's **Account** section;
- **on a tablet or computer**, your profile menu (top right);
- the [Household page](household.md)'s **On this device**.

With two or more people on the device, it also opens when the app starts. Turn that off with **Ask who's listening when the app opens** on the Household page.

## Adding someone

- **Someone without an account**, like a child: add them on the Household page with [Add someone to your household](household.md#household-members). It makes their account with no admin or invite.
- **Someone with an account**: tap **Add someone** on Who's listening? and sign in with their account, as when [connecting the app](connecting.md).

They become a new person on this device, and the app switches to them.

## Switching

When you switch, the other person's book pauses and its place is saved. The app goes to the new person's Home, with only their books, downloads and Up next. The [car](in-the-car.md) lists change too.

Nothing is lost: everyone's downloads stay on the device, and offline listening still syncs.

## A PIN for a person

Each person can have a four-digit **PIN**, asked whenever someone switches to them. Set it on the [Household page](household.md), under that person's **PIN**.

A PIN stays on this device and isn't your password. It keeps a sibling's taps away from your books, but isn't a lock against someone determined.

**Forgotten a PIN?** Remove that person from this device, then sign them in again. Their place in every book is on the server, so nothing is lost.

## On the Household page

![On this device on the Household page: a person's card with Switch, Name, PIN, Kids mode and Bedtime](/img/screenshots/web-player/household-device.png)

**On this device**, at the top of the [Household page](household.md), lists everyone here. For each person you can:

- **Switch** to them;
- rename them (on this device only);
- set a **PIN**;
- turn on [kids mode](kids-mode.md);
- **Sign in again** if their sign-in ended (see [When a member is signed out](household.md#when-a-member-is-signed-out-on-this-device));
- **Remove from this device**.

Removing someone signs them out here and deletes their downloads here. Their account and their place stay on the server. If they have no password, they'll need a new invite to sign in again, and the app warns you first.

## Good to know

- If the person listening is removed, the app asks **Who's listening?** straight away.
- A link to someone else's book on this device offers **Switch to** that person instead of opening it.
