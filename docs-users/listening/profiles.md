title: Sharing a device (profiles)
description: "Profiles: everyone who listens on one phone, tablet or computer keeps their own place in every book. Who's listening?, adding someone to the device, switching, the optional PIN, the Household page's On this device, removing someone, and links that belong to someone else."

A family tablet, a phone that gets handed round in the car, the computer in the kitchen: when more than one person listens on the same device, each of them can have a **profile** of their own. A profile is a person on this device. Each person signs in with their own AudioSilo account, so each keeps their own place in every book, their own [Up next](up-next.md), [bookmarks](bookmarks-and-notes.md) and [listening stats](you.md). The app shows one person's books and servers at a time: whoever is listening now.

If you were already using AudioSilo when profiles arrived, nothing changes until you add someone: everything you were signed in to is now your profile, and nobody was signed out.

## Who's listening?

**Who's listening?** shows everyone on this device as a large initial in a coloured circle, with a line under each name. The person listening now has a pink ring. Tap someone to switch to them, or **Close** to carry on as you are.

![Who's listening? on a computer: Ellen with the pink ring, Jamie with a key for the PIN, Leo in kids mode with the bedtime, Add someone, and Manage household at the foot](/img/screenshots/web-player/profiles.png)

Open it from:

- **on a phone**, the **Me** tab's **Account** section, under *Listening as*;
- **on a tablet or computer**, your [profile menu](browsing.md#getting-around) (top right), which also lists the other people to switch to straight away;
- the [Household page](household.md)'s **On this device**.

With two or more people on the device it also opens **when the app starts**, so whoever picks it up chooses first. Turn that off with **Ask who's listening when the app opens** on the Household page. It's turned on for you when a second person is added.

**Manage household** at the foot of Who's listening? opens the [Household page](household.md) (not while someone must be chosen, and not from kids mode).

## Adding someone

Someone who **doesn't have an account yet** (a child, or a partner who has never used AudioSilo) is easiest to add from the [Household page](household.md#household-members): **Add someone to your household** makes their account on your server, with no admin and no invite, and signs a kid in on this device straight away.

Someone who **already has an account**: tap **Add someone** on Who's listening?, or **Sign in someone who already has an account** on the Household page. They sign in with **their own account**, on your server or on another one, exactly as when [connecting a server](connecting.md). They become a new person on this device, and the app switches to them.

Each person can have one account on each server. If you sign in to a server where you already have an account, as a different user (a child's account on the family server, say), that account becomes a new person too, rather than replacing yours.

## Switching

When you switch to someone else:

- the book that was playing, if it's the other person's, is paused, its place saved, and put away, so the next person doesn't find someone else's book in the player;
- the app goes to the new person's Home page, with only their servers, books, downloads, Up next and stats;
- the [car](in-the-car.md) lists change to the new person's, and the iPhone widget clears until they play something.

Nothing of the other person is lost: their downloads stay on the device, and listening they did offline still syncs to their account in the background.

## A PIN for a person

Each person can have a **PIN**: four digits, asked whenever someone switches **to** that person. It's off by default. Set, change or remove it on the [Household page](household.md), under that person's **PIN**. Changing or removing a PIN asks for the current one first.

- A wrong PIN says *"That PIN doesn't match"*. After five wrong tries in a row, the prompt makes you wait 30 seconds.
- Leaving [kids mode](kids-mode.md) goes through Who's listening?, so whoever is chosen there is asked for their PIN.
- A PIN stays on this device: it isn't your account's password, and the server never sees it. It keeps a person's place and books away from a sibling's taps; it isn't a lock against someone determined to get in.

**Forgotten a PIN?** Remove that person from this device on the Household page (removing someone doesn't ask for their PIN), then sign in to their account again with **Add someone** (or, for a member of your household, **Sign in on this device** on their card). Their place in every book is on the server, so nothing is lost.

## On the Household page

![On this device on the Household page: Leo's card with Switch, Name, PIN, Kids mode and Bedtime, then Who's listening?, Sign in someone who already has an account, and Ask who's listening when the app opens](/img/screenshots/web-player/household-device.png)

**On this device**, at the top of the [Household page](household.md), lists everyone on this device. For each person:

- **Switch** to them (the person listening now is marked *Listening now*);
- **Name**: rename them. The name shows on this device only; their account keeps its username;
- **PIN**: set, change or remove it (above);
- **Kids mode**, with its library and bedtime (see [Kids mode](kids-mode.md));
- **Signed out**, with **Sign in again**, when their sign-in to a server has ended (see [When a member is signed out on this device](household.md#when-a-member-is-signed-out-on-this-device));
- **Remove from this device**: signs them out of their servers on this device and deletes their downloads here. Their place in every book stays on the server. If they have no password on a server, the app warns you first: they'll need a new invite to sign in there again. For a member of someone's household it says instead that their account stays, and who can sign them in here again from the Household page.

Below the people: **Who's listening?**, **Sign in someone who already has an account**, and the **Ask who's listening when the app opens** switch. Further down the page come the people you've added to [your household](household.md#household-members) and [Listening in the house](in-the-house.md#turning-it-on), one switch per server of the person listening.

Removing someone from this device never deletes their account. For a member of your household, **Delete their account** on their card does that: see [Deleting a member](household.md#deleting-a-member-or-removing-them-from-this-device).

## When the person listening is removed

If the person listening now is removed from the device, or signs out of their last server, and other people are left, the app asks **Who's listening?** straight away, and it can't be closed: nothing of anyone shows until someone is chosen (with their PIN, if they have one).

## Links to someone else's book

A link can belong to someone else on the device: the iPhone widget or Live Activity left showing their book, a page in the web player's history, a link someone shared. Instead of opening it, the app says **"This belongs to Sam"**, with **Switch to Sam** (asking for Sam's PIN if there is one) and **Go back**. After switching, the page opens where you are.

A link to a server this device is no longer signed in to says **"Not signed in to that server"**, with **Go back**.
