**Year in listening** tells your year as a short story, one card at a time: your hours, the books you finished, your book and narrator of the year, and more. It's made from your listening on your server. Nothing leaves the server unless you share a card.

To open it, go to **You** and choose **Year** on a phone or **Year in listening** on a tablet or computer (see [Finding You](you.md#finding-you)). **Play the story** at the bottom of [Your listening](you.md#your-listening) opens it too.

![Year in listening on a computer: the story card, the card thumbnails and Share this card](/img/screenshots/web-player/year.png)

## The cards

Each card appears only when it has something to say:

1. **Your hours** - how many hours you listened this year.
2. **Books finished** - how many, against your [yearly goal](you.md#your-yearly-goal).
3. **Your book of the year** - the book you spent the most time with.
4. **Your voice of the year** - the narrator you listened to most.
5. **When you listen** - your busiest time of day.
6. **Longest streak** - your longest run of days in a row.
7. **Who you kept coming back to** - your author and series of the year.
8. **Your year** - a summary, made for sharing.

A year with under an hour of listening and no finished book has no story yet.

## Playing the story

Each card shows for six seconds, then the next comes up.

- **Tap the right side** for the next card, **the left side** for the one before.
- **Hold your finger on a card** to pause it.
- **Tap a thumbnail** to jump to that card.
- In the web player, the **arrow keys** move between cards.

On a phone, tap **Play the story** to open it full screen. Close it with the **X** or by pulling it down.

If you use more than one server, a **Server** row picks whose year to show. A **Year** row lets you look back at earlier years.

## Sharing a card

1. Go to the card you want.
2. Tap **Share this card**.
3. Choose where to send it in the share sheet.

The card is shared as a phone-shaped picture. Where a browser can't share pictures, it downloads the picture instead. There's no link: only the picture you chose is shared.
