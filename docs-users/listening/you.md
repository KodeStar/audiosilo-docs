**You** is the part of AudioSilo about you rather than your library: how much you've listened, your year as a story, your [Journal](journal.md), your [settings](settings.md) and your [account](account.md).

## Finding You

**On a phone**, it's the **Me** tab at the bottom of the screen. A row of sections runs under its title: **Stats**, **Year**, **Journal**, **Settings** and **Account**. Swipe the row to see them all, and tap one to switch. The big title says where you are: *Your listening* over Stats, *Year in listening* over Year, and so on.

**On a tablet or computer**, it's **You** in the [top bar](browsing.md#getting-around). Its sections are **Stats**, **Year in listening** and **Journal**. Settings is the gear beside your profile button, and your account is in the profile menu.

In the web player's [quick search](browsing.md#quick-search-in-the-web-player), **Go to** lists **Your listening**, **Year in listening**, **Journal** and **Settings**.

## Your listening

**Stats** opens on *Your listening*: what you've listened to, drawn from your own listening on your server.

![Your listening on a computer: this week's hours, the stat tiles, the listening calendar, the listening clock and the hours per week](/img/screenshots/web-player/you-stats.png)

At the top, the server it comes from (*"Your listening · Hearthside"*, or just the server's name on a phone, under the big title) and how long you've listened **this week**. On a tablet or computer, **Open your 2026 story** beside it opens your [Year in listening](year-in-listening.md).

### One server at a time

Your listening is kept by each server for itself, so the page shows one server's: your main server's, to begin with. If you're signed in to two or more servers that keep listening stats, a row of their names above the page lets you switch.

Days and weeks follow **the server's clock**, not your device's: a session at 11:30 at night counts on the server's date for that moment, so the page reads the same on every device you use.

A server running an older AudioSilo doesn't keep listening stats. It says so (*"No listening stats here yet"*) instead of showing an empty page, and everything else works as usual.

### The tiles

| Tile | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **This week** | Your listening in the last seven days, and how that compares with the seven days before (*"↑ 2h 10m more than last week"*) |
| **Streak** | How many days in a row you've listened, counting today once you've played something (until then, up to yesterday: a streak isn't broken until the day is over). Underneath, your longest streak this year |
| **Goal** | Your yearly goal: books finished this year against the number you aim for, with a ring for how far you are |
| **Daily average** | How long you listen a day on average this year, counting the days you didn't listen too. Underneath, how many books you've listened to this year |

### Your yearly goal

A goal is a number of books to finish this year. Without one, the Goal tile says how many you've finished and offers **Set a yearly goal**, which sets one for you from your pace so far (at least a book a month, and always ahead of what you've already finished). Then:

- **-** and **+** lower and raise it (one book at a time up to 12, then in twos up to 40, then in fives).
- The **X** clears it.

Each change is saved on your server at once. It's the same goal as the ring on the home screen's [This week](browsing.md#the-home-screen) card, kept per server.

### The charts

Point at a chart on a computer, or tap it on a phone or tablet, to see the value under your finger. Each one also reads as a sentence to a screen reader.

- **Listening calendar** - every day of the last 12 months, darker on the days you listened more, with today outlined. It counts listening from every device, not just this one.
- **Your listening clock** - your listening this year by hour of the day, with your busiest hours picked out (*"Peaks at 9:00 PM-11:00 PM"*). The middle of the clock names your busiest hour.
- **Hours per week** - your last 12 weeks, with how much you listened in all.

### Your top authors, narrators and series

Three lists of up to five each, ranked by how long you listened to them this year: **Top authors**, **Top narrators** (*"You spent 31h with Kate Reading"*) and **Top series**. Tap a name to open that author's, narrator's or series page.

### Finished this year

The books you finished this year, as a row of spines. Tap one to open it. **See them in your Journal** opens your [Journal](journal.md).

At the bottom of the page, a card with your year so far (*"212h of listening and 23 books finished so far."*) and **Play the story** opens your [Year in listening](year-in-listening.md).

### On a phone

On a phone the same page sits in the Me tab, the tiles in two columns and the charts one under the other.

![Your listening on a phone, in the Me tab: the sections row, this week's hours and the stat tiles](/img/screenshots/web-player/phone-you-stats.png)

:::note
Until you've listened to something, the page says *"No listening yet"*: play a book and your week, your streak and your year fill in.
:::
