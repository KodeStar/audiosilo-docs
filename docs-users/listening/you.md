**Stats** shows how much you've listened: this week, your streak, your yearly goal, when you listen, and your top authors. It lives in **You**, with your [Year in listening](year-in-listening.md), [Journal](journal.md), [settings](settings.md), [household](household.md) and [account](account.md).

## Finding You

- **On a phone**: tap the **Me** tab. Swipe the row of sections under the title (**Stats**, **Year**, **Journal**, **Settings**, **Household**, **Account**, and **Upcoming** when your server has [upcoming releases](upcoming.md)) and tap one.
- **On a tablet or computer**: tap **You** in the [top bar](browsing.md#getting-around). Settings is the gear beside your profile button, and your account is in the profile menu.

## Your listening

**Stats** opens on *Your listening*, with how long you've listened this week at the top.

![Your listening on a computer: this week, the stat tiles, the listening calendar, clock and hours per week](/img/screenshots/web-player/you-stats.png)

The tiles show:

- **This week** - the last seven days, compared with the seven before.
- **Streak** - how many days in a row you've listened, and your longest this year.
- **Goal** - books finished this year against your goal.
- **Daily average** - how long you listen a day this year.

### Your yearly goal

A goal is a number of books to finish this year.

1. On the **Goal** tile, tap **Set a yearly goal**. It suggests one from your pace so far.
2. Use **-** and **+** to change it.
3. Tap the **X** to clear it.

It's the same goal as the ring on [Home](browsing.md#the-home-screen).

### The charts

Point at or tap a chart to see a value.

- **Listening calendar** - every day of the last 12 months, darker on the days you listened more.
- **Your listening clock** - when in the day you listen most.
- **Hours per week** - your last 12 weeks.

Below the charts are your **Top authors**, **Top narrators** and **Top series** this year. Tap a name to open its page.

### Finished this year

The books you finished this year, as a row of spines. Tap one to open it, or **See them in your Journal**.

![Your listening on a phone, in the Me tab: the sections row, this week and the stat tiles](/img/screenshots/web-player/phone-you-stats.png)

### One server at a time

Each server keeps its own stats. If you use more than one, a row of server names at the top lets you switch.

Days follow the server's clock, so the page reads the same on every device.
