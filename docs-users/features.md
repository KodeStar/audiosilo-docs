Everything AudioSilo can do, one line each. Features marked **(Bridge)** need the
optional [Bridge](getting-started/running-a-bridge.md) running beside your server.
Everything else works with the server alone.

## Listening

- [Play a book](listening/playback.md): start, pause and skip from any screen, and pick up where you left off on any device.
- [The full player](listening/full-player.md): chapters, time left, speed, Voice boost, and Smart speed on Android.
- [Listen offline](listening/offline-downloads.md): download books and podcast episodes to your phone, tablet or browser.
- [Sleep timer](listening/sleep-timer.md): stop after some minutes or chapters, and shake to keep going.
- [Bookmarks and notes](listening/bookmarks-and-notes.md): mark a moment with one tap and add a note.
- [Up next](listening/up-next.md): a queue of the books you want to hear next.
- [The end of a book](listening/end-of-book.md): end credits and the next book in the series.
- [Browse and search](listening/browsing.md): Home, the Library, Search and your favourites.
- [Collections](listening/browsing.md#collections): your own lists of books, such as "Road trip", which you can share.
- [The book page](listening/book-page.md): your place, chapters, bookmarks and details for one book.
- [The companion](listening/companion.md): characters, the story so far, the ebook text and bookmarks beside the player.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](listening/keyboard-shortcuts.md) in the web player.

## Reading and extras

- [Read the ebook](listening/reading-the-ebook.md): read a book's EPUB, with your place kept on every device.
- [Follow the narration](listening/reading-the-ebook.md#follow-the-narration) in the ebook, sentence by sentence, once an admin [makes a read-along](admin/making-read-along.md). **(Bridge)**
- [Recaps and characters](listening/recaps-and-characters.md): catch up without spoilers past where you are.
- [Upcoming releases](listening/upcoming.md): new and coming books in your series, with a calendar feed.
- [Podcasts](listening/podcasts.md): follow and play shows, download episodes, and import shows from another app. **(Bridge)**
- [Your listening stats](listening/you.md): time listened, streaks, goals and your top authors.
- [Your year in listening](listening/year-in-listening.md): a yearly story of cards you can share.
- [Journal](listening/journal.md): every day you listened, and all your bookmarks and notes.

## Apps and devices

- [Get the apps](listening/mobile-apps.md): the web player, iPhone and iPad, and Android.
- [Connect the app](listening/connecting.md): sign in with a QR code, an invite or a password.
- [Apple Watch](listening/apple-watch.md): send books to your watch and listen without your phone.
- [In the car](listening/in-the-car.md): Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.
- [Cast](listening/chromecast.md): play on a Chromecast, TV or Nest speaker.
- [App settings](listening/settings.md): playback, downloads, appearance and language.
- [Your account](listening/account.md): password, devices and listening sync.
- [API keys](listening/api-keys.md): let a dashboard or script reach your server.

## Household and sharing

- [Invite people](admin/users-and-invites.md): give someone their own account on your server.
- [Add your household](listening/household.md): add your kids and the adults you live with yourself.
- [Limit what someone can see](admin/sharing.md): share a whole library, an author, a series or one book.
- [Share a device](listening/profiles.md): several people on one phone or computer, each with their own place.
- [Kids mode](listening/kids-mode.md): a simple player with big covers and a bedtime.
- [See what others are listening to](listening/in-the-house.md): an opt-in row on Home.

## Your library

- [Organize your audiobook files](getting-started/organizing-your-library.md): folder layout and supported formats.
- [Libraries](admin/libraries.md): add folders, rescan and schedule scans.
- [Fix book details](admin/books.md): match books with community metadata, or edit them by hand.
- [Library health](admin/health.md): find books that could be better and match many at once.
- [Community metadata](community/meta-site.md): a free database of books, series and release dates.
- [Characters, recaps and chapters](admin/generating-characters-and-recaps.md) for books the community hasn't covered. **(Bridge)**
- [Back up your Audible books](admin/audible.md) and keep your places in step with Audible. **(Bridge)**
- [Contribute from your server](community/contributing-from-your-server.md): share corrections and recaps with the community. **(Bridge)**
- [Import from Audiobookshelf](admin/import.md): bring over listening history, progress and bookmarks.

## Running the server

- Install with [Docker](getting-started/quickstart-docker.md), on [Unraid](getting-started/install-unraid.md), or [without Docker](getting-started/install-binary.md).
- [Set up your server](getting-started/first-run.md): the first-run checklist.
- [Reach your server from outside your home](getting-started/remote-access.md): a domain name and a trusted certificate.
- [Back up your server](admin/backups.md): scheduled and manual backups, and restore.
- [Notifications](admin/notifications.md): new books, failed scans and sign-ins, on your phone, Discord or a webhook.
- [Activity](admin/activity.md): who is listening now, and listening over time.
- [Server settings](admin/server.md): every setting in the admin console, plus updates and logs.
- [The admin console](admin/console-tour.md): where everything lives.
- [The Bridge](getting-started/running-a-bridge.md): the optional companion that adds the **(Bridge)** features above.
- [Try the demo](demo.md) without installing anything.
